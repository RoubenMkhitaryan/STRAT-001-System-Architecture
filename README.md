# STRAT-001 — System Architecture & Quantitative Market Research

## Project Overview

STRAT-001 is an independent research and system-design project focused on the architecture of a modular platform for systematic cryptocurrency market analysis and trading simulations.

The project combines historical market data processing, rule-based research workflows, event-driven system logic, and reproducible validation procedures.

The primary objective was to design a structured research environment in which data processing, research logic, and execution-style simulations could be developed and evaluated consistently.

## System Architecture

The platform was organized around several functional components:

**1. Historical Data Layer**

Collection, storage, and structured processing of historical market data.

**2. Research Logic**

Implementation of rule-based research procedures and market-event identification.

**3. State and Event Processing**

Management of system states and sequential processing of market events.

**4. Simulation Layer**

Execution-style simulations designed to evaluate system behavior under historical conditions.

**5. Validation and Diagnostics**

Consistency checks, reproducibility controls, and diagnostic reporting.

The architecture emphasizes separation of responsibilities between components and traceability of research results.

## Validation Approach

A central design principle was ensuring that historical research results could be reproduced and independently checked.

The validation framework emphasized:

- Chronological processing of historical data.
- Prevention of future-data leakage into historical decisions.
- Consistent definitions of events and system states.
- Reproducible research procedures.
- Verification of intermediate and final outputs.
- Separation of research assumptions from implementation details.
